Here are some of the projects I have worked on as part of my learning and development.

Smart Pantry AI

Smart Pantry AI is an AI-powered pantry management system designed to help users manage their household inventory.

The system can scan bills, extract purchased items, store inventory information, track stock and expiry dates, provide low-stock reminders and suggest recipes based on available ingredients.

Key Features

  • Bill scanning
  • Item extraction using OCR
  • Inventory management
  • Stock tracking
  • Expiry tracking
  • Low-stock reminders
  • Recipe suggestions

Technologies

Python, AI, OCR and SQLite
